ESP32-C3 RGBIC LED Ring

A programmable circular RGBIC LED development board for lighting effects, electronics learning and DIY projects.

24 RGBIC LEDs ESP32-C3 Wi-Fi & Bluetooth USB Type-C

01. Product Overview

Get to know your ESP32-C3 RGBIC LED Ring Development Board.

Board layout and component identification

Main Controller

ESP32-C3 MCU with integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

RGBIC Lighting

24 onboard WS2812B addressable RGBIC LEDs.

Control Buttons

RESET button and BOOT function button.

GPIO Expansion

IO0 and IO1 for custom external connections.

02. Key Features

Built for convenient operation and further development.

?

Five Built-In Lighting Effects

  • Factory-preloaded demo firmware.
  • Press the BOOT button to switch between five lighting effects.
  • Ready to use after power-up.
?

ESP32-C3 Wireless Connectivity

  • Integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.
  • USB Type-C programming interface.
  • No separate programmer required.
?

Two GPIO Pins for Expansion

  • IO0 and IO1 available for external connections.
  • Connect sensors or communicate with another microcontroller.
  • Suitable for custom development.
?

Convenient Board Design

  • RESET button for restarting the board.
  • BOOT button for switching demo effects.
  • Mounting hole for enclosure installation.
Important: The factory-installed demo firmware does not include app-based remote control. App connectivity and network-based remote control require user-developed applications and firmware.

03. Technical Specifications

Hardware specifications provided for this board.

ItemSpecification
Main ControllerESP32-C3
Wireless ConnectivityWi-Fi + Bluetooth
Power InterfaceUSB Type-C
Operating Voltage5 V
Typical Operating Current200 mA
Exposed GPIOIO0, IO1 (3.3 V logic)
LEDs24 × WS2812B addressable RGBIC LEDs
Board Diameter6.5 cm
ButtonsRESET and BOOT
GPIO Logic Level3.3 V
GPIO Voltage Warning: GPIO pins use 3.3 V logic. Do not connect 5 V signals directly to IO0, IO1 or other GPIO connections.
